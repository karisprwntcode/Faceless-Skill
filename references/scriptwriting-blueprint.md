# The HPMR Scriptwriting & Retention Blueprint (30s – 60s)

Every script generated by this skill must follow the **Hook - Pain - Mechanism - Reward (HPMR)** framework to maximize completion rate and algorithmic watch-time.

---

## 🎬 The 4-Act Retention Architecture

```
[00:00 - 00:03] ACT 1: THE HOOK        ➔ Break a myth / Counter-intuitive question (Character Shocked)
[00:03 - 00:10] ACT 2: THE PAIN        ➔ Show the real danger & consequence (Character Confused / Metaphor)
[00:10 - 00:22] ACT 3: THE MECHANISM   ➔ Visual abstraction + practical code (Character Coding / Terminal)
[00:22 - 00:30] ACT 4: THE REWARD      ➔ Stat benchmark + Seamless Loop bridge (Character Eureka 💡)
```

### Act 1: The Hook (Frames 0 – 90 / 0–3s)
- **Goal**: Stop the scroll within 1.5 seconds.
- **Copywriting Formula**: *"Kebanyakan orang mengira [Mitos Umum], padahal [Fakta Menyeramkan] yang bikin [Masalah Fatal]!"*
- **Visuals**:
  - Immediate bold text + `<StabiloHighlight>` on the controversy word.
  - `<CharacterFigure persona="..." emotion="shocked" />`.
  - SFX: Soft sub-bass thud.

### Act 2: The Pain (Frames 90 – 240 / 3–8s)
- **Goal**: Make the audience feel the friction and cost of the problem.
- **Copywriting Formula**: *"Setiap kali [Aksi Biasa], sistem kamu diam-diam [Efek Kerusakan] sampai akhirnya [Bencana Terjadi]."*
- **Visuals**:
  - Visual metaphor (e.g. `<MemoryLeakVisualizer />` or `<FlowDiagram />` with red bottleneck).
  - Background mood: `<AuroraMesh mood="tense" />`.
  - Floating dust particles: `<DustParticles />`.

### Act 3: The Mechanism (Frames 240 – 360 / 8–12s)
- **Goal**: Deliver the "Aha!" moment with undeniable technical clarity.
- **Copywriting Formula**: *"Solusinya bukan beli hardware mahal, tapi ganti pola ini ke [Solusi Nyata]."*
- **Visuals**:
  - Side-by-side `<ComparisonCard />` or `<CodeTerminal />` with highlighted fix line.
  - `<CursorPointer />` gliding and clicking on the exact line of code.
  - `<CharacterFigure action="holding-laptop" />`.

### Act 4: The Reward & Seamless Loop (Frames 360 – 450 / 12–15s+)
- **Goal**: Prove the outcome with numbers, then loop back into the hook.
- **Copywriting Formula**: *"Hasilnya? [Metrik Terukur seperti Latency Drop 99%]. Dan itulah rahasia di balik..."*
- **Visuals**:
  - `<StatCounter />` rolling up to target value with emerald glow.
  - `<CharacterFigure emotion="eureka" />` with glowing lightbulb `💡`.
  - **Looping Bridge**: The ending phrase syntactically completes the sentence when replayed at Act 1!
